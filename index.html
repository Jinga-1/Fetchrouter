How to read a card

A quick version of the conventions below — read the full guide for search, filters, admin, and publishing.

01The number is not the grade

A $200 claim that nobody could confirm ranks below a $70 grant that works. Sorting defaults to rating, never to the dollar figure.

02Publish the doubt

Every entry carries a verdict — verified, unverified, or disputed — with the same visual weight as the credit figure, and unverifiable offers are ranked accordingly.

03Cost the click honestly

See what a signup actually costs — a GitHub OAuth grant, an account-age rule, a coding-agent-only restriction — next to what it pays.
